# The statement's contrast while dimmed

Status: ready-for-human
Audit: A6 (WCAG 1.4.3)

## Scope

`ScrollWords` shows the homepage statement at 16 % opacity and lights each word as the
reader reaches it; while dimmed the words fail contrast. Visitors who ask for less motion
see it lit. A design decision: keep the effect as it is, raise the starting opacity
(still failing below about 60 %), or light the whole line once it is in view.

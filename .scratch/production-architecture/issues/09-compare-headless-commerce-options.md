# Compare headless commerce options for hybrid sales

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none
Map: ../map.md

## Question

Using current primary documentation, how do Shopify headless, Medusa, and a focused Stripe-backed custom commerce module compare for later hybrid Sales Modes where Customer Accounts and Projects remain application-owned, configured kitchens are quote- or deposit-led, and standardized products may use direct checkout?

## Answer

[The primary-source comparison](../research/headless-commerce-options.md) recommends deferring a commerce platform until a paid Sales Mode is approved: use Stripe for a narrow deposit/accepted-quote tracer bullet, Shopify for committed direct retail and merchant operations, and Medusa only when strategic custom workflows justify its additional production stack.
